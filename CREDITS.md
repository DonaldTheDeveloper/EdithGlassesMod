# Credits and third-party material

## 3D model

The glasses model is a voxel (block) conversion of **"Tony Stark EDITH Eye Glasses"** by **Irfan Ma'ruf**,
downloaded from [BlenderKit](https://www.blenderkit.com/) (asset id `864cabea-9db9-419b-ae37-2556695d2b44`,
Royalty Free licence). The original model file is not part of this repository. The block models in
`src/main/resources/assets/edithglasses/models/item/` are an approximation generated by `tools/gen_model_from_glb.py`
from the original, using materials from BlenderKit's "Leather Fabric 01" (Julio Sillet).

The logo and banner in `logo/` are renders of that model.

## Inspiration

The look and feel of the glasses is inspired by the E.D.I.T.H. glasses from the Marvel films. This is a fan project,
not affiliated with or endorsed by Marvel or Disney. No film footage, audio or artwork is included.

## Services

EDITH can talk to AI services (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek, xAI, Cerebras,
Together AI, or a local Ollama server) using API keys that the player supplies. Those services have their own terms.
No keys are included in this repository.
